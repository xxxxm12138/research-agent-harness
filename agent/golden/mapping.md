## What To Imitate

- Start from the decision and the core question, not from a company list.
- Define what the label includes and excludes.
- Use axes that change the investment conclusion.
- Keep evidence and judgment adjacent.
- Mark information status and missing DD items.
- End with an action (Push, Watch, Pass, DD or Pitch angle) when the intent asks for one.
- For `mapping 底稿`, imitate the disciplined structure without forcing a final recommendation: classification logic, a complete table, institution portraits, project profiles and a source appendix.

## What Not To Imitate Blindly

- Do not copy old dates, valuation numbers or company status without verification.
- Do not reuse one track's axes if they fail to separate the current players.
- Do not quote confidential deal details into shared drafts.
- Do not turn a calibrated sample into a rigid template. Preserve the intent rules first, then adapt the structure to the new track.
